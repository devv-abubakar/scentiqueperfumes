Our Story

Made slowly,
in Lahore.

Scentique began in 2019 with a single bench, a set of borrowed pipettes, and an argument about whether oud could be worn in daylight. It can — at the right concentration, beside the right citrus.

We buy our absolutes directly: Turkish rose from Isparta, sandalwood from certified Australian plantations, vetiver from Haiti. Every batch is macerated for sixty days, filtered twice, and bottled by hand in lots of two hundred.

Nothing is rushed, and nothing is copied. If a formula doesn't hold on skin for eight hours, it doesn't get a name.
